01 Discover & Plan

Solid preparation is the backbone of successful BIM delivery. At WorqGroup, we believe that every high-performing project starts with strategic planning, technical clarity, and team alignment. Our specialists take the time to fully understand your project’s goals, scope, technical requirements, and programme — ensuring that no detail is overlooked before modelling even begins.

Whether you're at concept stage or preparing for execution, we work closely with your design, engineering, and delivery teams to define expectations, structure workflows, and map out a digital strategy that supports accuracy and efficiency across the entire project lifecycle. Our planning support is grounded in experience, collaboration, and a genuine commitment to getting it right the first time.

    03 Construction and build process

    Supporting your construction phase with hands-on BIM expertise. At WorqGroup, we don’t just hand over a model — we stay actively engaged throughout the build to ensure your project runs smoothly from the first pour to final handover. Our construction-savvy team of BIM coordinators, engineers, and trades-informed detailers provide on-the-ground support to help your site teams interpret, coordinate, and execute the design with confidence.

    We deliver more than just clean geometry — our models are fully coordinated, clash-resolved, and ready for real-world installation. During construction, we remain a hands-on partner, assisting with real-time model updates, onsite BIM support, and live coordination troubleshooting to minimise rework and keep your programme on track.

    Our services also include as-built model verification, ensuring that the final digital record accurately reflects site conditions for handover and future use. With ongoing BIM modelling production tailored to your evolving site needs, we help streamline communication between design and construction.

    At WorqGroup, we build with you — not just for you — to deliver coordinated, constructible results that meet the highest standards of quality, efficiency, and delivery certainty.

    04 Post handover and Operations

    At WorqGroup, we support our clients well beyond construction, ensuring that every project transitions smoothly into its operational phase with a clear, comprehensive digital handover.
