Hotel RIVA is a hospitality project located in Scotland, conceived through a comprehensive integration of architectural design, structural systems and MEP coordination. The project emphasizes spatial clarity, functional efficiency and technical precision, supported by a strategic space management approach. Its design responds to the local context while ensuring long-term operational performance, flexibility and a refined guest experience aligned with contemporary hospitality standards.
