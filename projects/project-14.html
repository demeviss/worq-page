MENA Narin is a large-scale hospitality project currently under construction, developed through an integrated architectural, structural and MEP approach supported by comprehensive BIM and project management workflows. With a gross area of 32,000 square meters, the project emphasizes spatial efficiency, coordinated technical systems and long-term operational performance. A strategic space management framework guides the organization of public and private areas, ensuring clarity of circulation, functional flexibility and a cohesive architectural identity throughout the hotel.
