    YACH Interior Promotech

    In Cooperation with Promotechh

    Building Type

    Yacht Interio

    Gross Area

    98m2

    Status

    Completed

    Located within a large green belt in Surrey, this project replaced a series of outdated and inefficient office buildings with a single, consolidated two-storey modern office facility. The design vision focused on unifying the site into one coherent architectural volume while meeting current building energy-efficiency legislation. Set within a historic landscape and surrounded by greenery, the building was conceived to provide multi-aspect views from the internal office spaces, creating a distinctive and high-quality working environment. A large atrium defines the main entrance, leading into a warm reception space and a sculptural white staircase that complements the building’s interior palette. Passive design strategies were integrated throughout, enabling future renewable energy implementation and long-term performance adaptability, supported by future-proofing elements such as in-situ cast concrete floors and clerestory glazing.
