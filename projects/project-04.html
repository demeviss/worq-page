Located in Sardinia, Italy, this private villa was designed as a contemporary interpretation of Mediterranean living, where architecture and lighting design are carefully orchestrated to enhance spatial atmosphere and visual continuity. Developed in partnership with Promotech, the project prioritizes openness, natural light and a strong dialogue between interior spaces and the surrounding landscape. Material restraint, precise proportions and a refined lighting strategy define a residence that balances comfort, elegance and architectural clarity within its coastal context.
