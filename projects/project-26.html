This large-scale hotel and commercial development was conceived through a comprehensive concept design strategy aimed at creating a dynamic mixed-use destination. Spanning a gross area of 110,000 square metres, the project explores the integration of hospitality and commercial functions within a cohesive architectural vision. The design focuses on spatial clarity, flexibility and future adaptability, establishing a strong identity while responding to the urban context and long-term development potential.
