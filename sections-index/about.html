    Our Journey

    Welcome to the visionary world of Worq Group, where innovation meets excellence. Founded in 2014 by Jonathan and Sadegh, hailing from Dublin and London, our journey began under the banner of Eblana Group.

    Drawing upon our blend of business acumen and design expertise, we pioneered ventures in design and construction material trading, setting the stage for transformative creations.

    The Highest Standards

    Worq is a multifaceted entity encompassing architectural design, construction, and hospitality management with unparalleled finesse. We design and manage structures from hotels and villas to industrial factories and residential complexes.

    Innovation, Efficiency & Sustainability

    Through Building Information Modeling (BIM), we orchestrate every aspect of our creations, ensuring aesthetic excellence, structural integrity, and operational optimisation.

    Where We Are

    Beginning in Dublin, our journey has expanded to London, Glasgow, and Dubai — marking our global footprint and commitment to diverse markets.
